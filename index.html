Most businesses that want to grow don't need more things to do. They need to know which few changes will actually matter. INCRMNTL finds those, then helps make them.

INCRMNTL

The name is the method. Four stages — Insight, Commercial, Marketing, Total Leverage — in the order a business should take them.

IN

Insight

Understand the business before changing it.

  • Website & Shopify review
  • Analytics & conversion
  • Customer journey
  • Positioning & competitors
  • Opportunity mapping
  • Prioritised recommendations

Outcome Know where growth is being lost, and where the best opportunities sit. Delivered as a fixed-fee review, a workshop and report, or a 30/60/90-day plan.

CR

Commercial

Improve the economics and the offer.

  • Pricing strategy
  • Offer structure
  • Margin review
  • Packaging & bundling
  • Product & service mix
  • Revenue model

Outcome Improve what the business sells, how it sells it, and the value it keeps. Run as a commercial strategy sprint.

MN

Marketing

Build or improve the growth engine.

  • Fractional marketing leadership
  • Channel strategy
  • Campaign planning
  • CRO
  • Lifecycle & retention
  • Agency management
  • Team training

Outcome A repeatable system for generating and converting demand — not a campaign that works once.

TL

Total Leverage

Embedded transformation across the whole business.

  • End-to-end growth strategy
  • Business operating system
  • Growth roadmap
  • Systems & reporting
  • Marketing leadership
  • Cross-functional delivery

Outcome Several levers pulled together, long enough to change the trajectory of the business. Performance-linked and hybrid arrangements where they make sense.

Why the order matters

IN → CR → MN → TL

The sequence isn't a menu, it's an argument. Spending on marketing before the pricing is right just buys more customers at the wrong margin — you get busier and no better off.

So the work starts with a diagnosis, fixes what the business sells and what it keeps, and only then builds the engine that brings more of it in. Most engagements start at IN for exactly that reason.

Start with the diagnosis.

If you know the business could perform better but not which changes would matter most, that's the conversation worth having. It starts with a look at what you've already got.
